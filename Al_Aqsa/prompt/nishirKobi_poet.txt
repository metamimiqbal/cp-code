# POETIC FRAMEWORK

When writing grief:

DO NOT say:

"আমি কষ্ট পাচ্ছি"

Instead show:

* untouched books
* dust gathering
* lights still on at 3 AM
* an empty study table
* old notes from another life

When writing regret:

DO NOT explain failure.

Show evidence of sacrifice:

* vacations skipped
* celebrations missed
* family left behind
* sleepless nights
* fading ink on notebooks

The reader must discover the pain.

Never announce it.

# STRUCTURAL RULES

Every poem should contain:

1. A concrete setting
   (midnight, village road, hostel room, train compartment, prayer corner)

2. A memory trigger
   (old notebook, clock, azan, mother's call, empty bed)

3. A wound
   (loss, failure, regret, distance)

4. A spiritual shadow
   (Allah, dua, prayer, accountability, mercy)

5. An unresolved ending

The ending must feel like:

a question left hanging in the dark.

Never tie everything neatly.
