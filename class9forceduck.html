What is Force?

Force is an interaction that can change the motion of an object — it can cause acceleration, deceleration, or deformation. In SI units force is measured in newtons (N): 1 N = 1 kg·m/s².

Key definition: Force is a vector quantity with magnitude and direction. Represented commonly as F. SI unit: newton (N).

Everyday examples

  • Push or pull on a door
  • Friction between shoes and ground
  • Gravitational pull of the Earth (weight)

Animated concept

Observe acceleration formula a = (F - f)/m where f = μ·N

Vectors & Representation

Force must be shown with magnitude and direction. We add forces vectorially using head-to-tail method or components (x,y).

Component method

Resolve a force into perpendicular components. For a force F at angle θ: Fx = F cosθ, Fy = F sinθ.

Example: If F = 20 N at 30°, Fx = 20 cos30° ≈ 17.32 N, Fy ≈ 10 N.

Newton's Laws of Motion

  1. First law (Inertia): A body remains at rest or in uniform motion unless acted upon by a net external force.
  2. Second law: Net force = mass × acceleration (ΣF = m a).
  3. Third law: For every action there is an equal and opposite reaction.
Use ΣF = m a in component form: ΣFx = m ax, ΣFy = m ay.

Friction

Friction is a resistive force that opposes motion (or attempted motion). Two main types: static friction (fs ≤ μs N) and kinetic friction (fk = μk N).

Typical relation: fk = μk N. Static friction adjusts up to a maximum μs N.

Work, Energy & Power

Work done by a constant force F along displacement s: W = F·s·cosθ. Kinetic energy KE = 1/2 m v². Work–energy theorem: Net work = ΔKE. Power P = W/t = F·v.

Remember: Work is scalar. Positive when force has component along displacement.

Worked Examples (step-by-step)

Example 1 — Box on floor

A 10 kg box is pushed with horizontal force 40 N. Friction coefficient μk = 0.2. Find acceleration.
1) Normal N = mg = 10×9.8 = 98 N
2) Friction fk = μk N = 0.2×98 = 19.6 N
3) Net horizontal force = 40 − 19.6 = 20.4 N
4) Acceleration = Fnet/m = 20.4/10 = 2.04 m/s²

Example 2 — Inclined plane

Find minimum μs to keep a 5 kg block at rest on a 30° incline.
Component down slope = mg sin30 = 5×9.8×0.5 = 24.5 N.
Normal = mg cos30 = 5×9.8×0.866 = 42.4 N.
μs(min) = (mg sinθ)/N = tanθ = tan30 = 0.577 → μs ≥ 0.577.
